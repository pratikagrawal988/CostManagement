KEY STRUCTURAL CHANGES:
────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

1. ROUTES ORGANIZATION
   Before: routes_cost.py, routes_aws.py, routes_azure.py, routes_gcp.py (flat)
   After:  api/v1/{cost,aws,azure,gcp}/routes.py (organized by resource)

2. JOBS ORGANIZATION
   Before: jobs.py, azure_jobs.py, gcp_jobs.py (flat)
   After:  jobs/{scheduler.py,aws/,azure/,gcp/,shared/} (organized by CSP)

3. API VERSIONING
   Before: /api/cost, /api/ingest-config (unversioned)
   After:  /api/v1/cost, /api/v1/aws/ingest-config (versioned)

4. SERVICES LAYER
   Before: evaluator.py, recommendations.py in root
   After:  services/{evaluator.py,recommendation_service.py} (organized)

5. SEED DATA
   Before: seed.py, seed_cost_mappings.py (scattered)
   After:  seed/{seed_data.py,mappings/service_mappings.py} (organized)

6. ROOT DIRECTORIES
   Before: Recommendation-engine/backend, Recommendation-engine/frontend
   After:  backend/, frontend/, infrastructure/, docs/ (standard layout)
